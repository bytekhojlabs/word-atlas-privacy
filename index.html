Privacy Policy for Word Atlas

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Word Atlas ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Word Atlas is designed to be a privacy-friendly game experience. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal identification information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to play the game.

  • Gameplay Progress & Data: Your level progress, coins, museum relics, statistics, and game settings are saved locally on your device.
  • Device Information: Standard, non-identifying technical information (such as operating system version and device model) may be automatically processed by the Android operating system solely to ensure application stability and proper screen rendering.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Word Atlas does not track your location, access your contacts, or sell personal data to third parties. If standard platform utilities (such as Google Play Services or Google Play Billing) are integrated, they operate under Google's own privacy standards to handle secure transactions and app updates.

3. Children's Privacy

Word Atlas is a general-audience educational trivia and crossword game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Sharing and Transfer

Because we do not collect personal user data on external servers, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties or advertising brokers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.
